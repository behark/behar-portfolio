# Marketing Materials Guide

## 📄 Flyer (A4 Size)

**Location**: `/public/flyer.html`

### How to Use:
1. Open `flyer.html` in your browser
2. Press `Ctrl+P` (or Cmd+P on Mac) to print
3. Select "Save as PDF" or print directly
4. Print on A4 paper (210mm × 297mm)

### Design Features:
- Professional blue gradient background
- Clear value propositions
- Pricing information
- Contact details
- Trust badges

### Customization:
- Update contact information
- Add your actual website URL
- Add QR code (you can generate one at qr-code-generator.com)
- Change colors to match your brand

---

## 💳 Business Card (85mm × 55mm)

**Location**: `/public/business-card.html`

### How to Use:
1. Open `business-card.html` in your browser
2. Press `Ctrl+P` (or Cmd+P on Mac)
3. In print settings:
   - Select "More settings"
   - Set "Pages per sheet" to 10 (for A4 paper)
   - Set margins to "None" or "Minimum"
4. Print on business card paper (or regular paper and cut)

### Standard Business Card Size:
- **Width**: 85mm (3.35 inches)
- **Height**: 55mm (2.17 inches)

### Print Settings:
- **Paper**: Business card paper (or thick cardstock)
- **Layout**: 10 cards per A4 page (2 columns × 5 rows)
- **Cutting**: Use a paper cutter for clean edges

### Design Features:
- Front: Name, title, contact info
- Back: Services list (optional)
- Professional gradient design
- Easy to read contact information

## 🖨️ Printing Tips

### Flyer:
- **Paper**: 200-250gsm for durability
- **Finish**: Glossy for vibrant colors, matte for professional look
- **Quantity**: Print 100-500 for local distribution

### Business Cards:
- **Paper**: 300-350gsm cardstock
- **Finish**: Matte or glossy
- **Corners**: Rounded corners look more professional
- **Quantity**: Start with 500-1000 cards

---

## 📍 Distribution Strategy

### Flyers:
1. **Local Businesses**: Visit shops, restaurants, cafes
2. **Community Centers**: Libraries, community boards
3. **Events**: Local markets, business events
4. **Direct Mail**: Send to potential clients

### Business Cards:
1. **Networking Events**: Always carry 10-20 cards
2. **Client Meetings**: Leave card after meetings
3. **Local Shops**: Ask to leave cards at reception
4. **Referrals**: Give to satisfied clients for referrals
